About this prototype

KARU TRACK

A cinematic campus discovery network for Karatina University: find participating students nearby, discover approved campus places, and share your location only on your terms.

Status
Design prototype · v0.9
Data
Simulated students, illustrative map
Planned stack
React · Vite · Tailwind · Framer · Leaflet · Supabase
Compliance target
Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 (ODPC)
  • Only participating students can ever appear on the map.
  • Sharing is off by default with a one-tap stop and auto-expiry.
  • Approximate locations are the default; precise sharing is temporary.
  • Blocking, reporting, and consent withdrawal are built in.
Prototype for demonstration · map artwork is illustrative, not to scale.
